Privacy Policy

Threads Monitor Bot
Last updated: July 19, 2026

Operator contact: honeycakeend@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how Threads Monitor Bot (“the App”, “we”) collects, uses, and stores information when you use our Telegram bot that searches and monitors public Threads posts via the official Meta Threads API.

1. What the App does

The App helps users monitor conversations on Threads by keyword or phrase. Users can run one-off searches and subscribe to recurring checks that send Telegram notifications when new matching posts appear.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Telegram data: Telegram user ID, chat ID, and messages you send to the bot (commands and search phrases).
  • Search and monitoring data: keyword/phrase pool items, poll interval settings, and identifiers of posts already seen or notified (to avoid duplicate alerts).
  • Threads / Meta platform data: data returned by the Threads API for keyword search, such as post ID, text, username, permalink, timestamp, and related public post metadata. Access is granted through Meta OAuth / access tokens with permissions such as threads_basic and threads_keyword_search.
  • Technical logs: application logs needed to operate and debug the service (for example API errors), which may include chat IDs or phrase text.

We do not intentionally collect payment card data or government IDs.

3. How we use information

  • To perform keyword / topic searches on Threads on your behalf.
  • To send Telegram notifications about new matching posts.
  • To remember your monitoring preferences and avoid duplicate alerts.
  • To maintain security (for example allowlists of Telegram user IDs).
  • To improve reliability and fix errors.

We use Threads platform data only for the purposes allowed by Meta’s Platform Terms and the approved use of threads_keyword_search (helping users manage social media presence by finding and reviewing keyword-related public content).

4. Legal basis / consent

You choose to use the App and grant required Telegram and Meta/Threads permissions. You can stop using the App and request deletion of your data at any time (see Data Deletion Instructions).

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. We may share data only with:

  • Meta / Threads — when calling the official Threads API with your authorized access token.
  • Telegram — to deliver bot messages and notifications.
  • Service providers strictly necessary to host the App (for example a VPS or cloud host), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities if required by applicable law.

6. Storage and retention

Data is stored in a local application database (SQLite) on the server that runs the bot. We retain monitoring phrases, chat settings, and seen-post identifiers while your monitoring remains active or until you request deletion. Logs are kept only as long as needed for operations and troubleshooting, then discarded or rotated.

7. Security

Access tokens and secrets are stored in server environment configuration, not in the public repository. Access to the bot may be restricted to allowlisted Telegram user IDs. No security measure is perfect; please contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

  • Access to the data we hold about your use of the App
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data (see Data Deletion Instructions)
  • Withdrawal of Meta/Threads authorization via Meta account settings

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. International transfers

Depending on where the bot is hosted and where Meta/Telegram process data, information may be processed in countries other than your own. Those services apply their own privacy terms.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

12. Contact
